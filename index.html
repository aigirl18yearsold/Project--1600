How to use this platform

1. Choose a SAT subject and skill.

2. Answer without looking up the solution.

3. Read the explanation—even when correct.

Start with short sets. Accuracy and careful review matter more than rushing through questions.

About 1600 Project

Our mission

Make structured SAT preparation more accessible.

1600 Project is an independent, free learning companion created to help students practice, understand mistakes, and track their own progress—especially students who may not have access to paid tutoring.

What we believe

  • Learning resources should be accessible on phones and low-cost devices.
  • Students deserve honest feedback, not inflated score predictions.
  • Understanding an error is more valuable than collecting a streak.
  • Official test information should come from official providers.

Transparency

This version contains a small set of original, SAT-style practice questions. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by College Board, Khan Academy, or Schoolhouse.world. It does not reproduce official SAT questions, simulate the complete adaptive algorithm, or calculate official scaled scores.

Privacy

No account is required. Practice history is stored in local browser storage on the device you use. The site does not intentionally send practice answers to a server. If you clear browser data, you may lose your history; use the export feature to make a backup. External resource links have their own privacy policies.

For a public launch, add your GitHub repository link, a contact method you control, and a contribution guide here. Do not publish private contact information.
